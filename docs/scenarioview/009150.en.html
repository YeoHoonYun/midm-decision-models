Key risks and checks

Short- and medium-term trends may diverge. Small analogue samples make historical path shares unstable. Volatility does not indicate future direction. News, disclosures and earnings events are absent from these inputs and cannot be asserted as causes of price movements.

Price changes after the data date are excluded. Provider-adjusted prices and corporate-action handling have not been independently audited. Historical stock analogues are not equivalent to validated index performance.

Company selection basis
